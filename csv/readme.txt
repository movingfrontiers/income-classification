THE SIX ASSUMPTIONS
Each one sits in a labelled cell on the Assumptions sheet and drives the projection through
formulas. Change a cell and everything downstream re-computes.

1. Starting level. Each economy starts from its latest reported GNI per capita, Atlas method,
   used exactly as published. No value is adjusted to fit the band implied by its 2025 class.

2. Growth rate. The median of the ten annual growth rates over the decade to 2025, that is the
   changes 2015-16 through 2024-25. A median rather than a compound rate, so that a single
   devaluation year or one-off spike cannot set a twenty-five year path.

3. Threshold drift. One common rate of 1.244 percent a year for all three thresholds. The Bank
   indexes all three to the same SDR deflator, and across 1990 to 2025 the ratios between them
   have held constant at 3.96 and 3.09. The rate is the median annual increase over the decade
   to 2025, which is close to invariant to the window chosen: 1.16 to 1.25 percent on any
   window from ten to twenty-five years.

4. Floor of 1.244 percent, equal to the drift. An economy therefore cannot lose ground against
   the threshold below it, and no economy is downgraded anywhere in the projection. This is
   verified across all 218 economies and all 25 years. It binds for 37 economies.

5. Cap at the 90th percentile of decade-median growth within each income group: 5.59 percent
   for low income, 7.47 for lower-middle, 9.07 for upper-middle and 7.17 for high income. No
   economy is projected to outgrow the top decile of its own group. It binds for 21 economies.

6. Freeze. An economy with fewer than 8 of the 10 annual observations is held at its 2025 class
   for the whole projection. This applies to 21 economies, of which six are not high income:
   Cuba, Eritrea, Korea Dem. Rep., South Sudan, Syrian Arab Republic and Yemen Rep.

Together the floor and the cap set 58 of the 197 projected growth rates. Every affected economy
is named in the Adjustment and Status columns of the Growth calculation sheet.

COVERAGE
The OGHIST worksheet lists 226 rows and 224 parse into this workbook, every one of them
classified in at least one year since 1987. Six left the classification before 2025 and are not
projected: the USSR, Yugoslavia, Czechoslovakia, Serbia and Montenegro, the Netherlands
Antilles and Mayotte. That leaves 218 economies classified in 2025, of which 197 are projected
forward and 21 are held at their 2025 class under assumption 6. The 197 carry 9.28 billion of
the 9.48 billion people in the 2050 total, or 97.8 percent. See the Coverage sheet.
